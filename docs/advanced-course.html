QM Advanced: Course overview

QM Advanced brings statistical modelling and research design together. You will choose methods that match the outcome, the structure of the data, and the strength of the claim you want to make.

By the end of QM Advanced, you can
  • choose a model that matches a continuous or binary outcome;
  • interpret moderation, mediation, and conditional effects;
  • use logistic regression for dummy outcomes and communicate results as probabilities or odds;
  • reason about panel data, fixed effects, difference-in-differences, and clustered uncertainty;
  • evaluate whether a research design supports descriptive, associational, or causal language;
  • connect a thesis question to a defensible analysis plan in R.

How this course connects

The same habits continue: define the estimand, understand the data-generating process, examine assumptions, interpret uncertainty, and qualify the claim. Advanced methods add flexibility, but they do not repair a poorly defined question or unsuitable data.

Tutorials and assessment

Two tutorials—one near the middle and one at the end of the course—provide integrated work with data in R and prepare you for the TestVision assessment. The end-course tutorial also asks you to connect design, model, output, and claim as you will need to do in independent research.
